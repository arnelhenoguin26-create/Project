  <h2>Welcome to Thailand</h2>
  <p>Thailand is a top travel destination known for its golden temples, peaceful Buddhist culture, and welcoming atmosphere. Over 90% of the population practices Theravada Buddhism, making temples an important part of everyday life and a must-see attraction for tourists.</p>
  <p>Visitors are welcome to explore these sacred places as long as they follow basic etiquette. This guide helps tourists enjoy Thailand’s religious sites comfortably, respectfully, and meaningfully.</p>
  <img src="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/0b/Grand_Palace_Bangkok.jpg" alt="Grand Palace Bangkok">
  <p class="photo-details">The Grand Palace complex in Bangkok is home to several sacred buildings and is often the first stop for tourists exploring Thailand’s religious heritage.</p>

<section id="sites">
  <h2>Must-Visit Religious Sites in Bangkok</h2>

  <h3>Wat Phra Kaew (Temple of the Emerald Buddha)</h3>
  <img src="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/8/89/Temple_of_the_Emerald_Buddha_Bangkok.jpg" alt="Wat Phra Kaew">
  <p class="photo-details">This image shows Wat Phra Kaew inside the Grand Palace. It is Thailand’s most sacred temple and houses the Emerald Buddha, carved from a single piece of jade. Tourists admire its detailed architecture and golden spires.</p>
  <img src="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/4c/Phra_Kaew_Mural_Bangkok.jpg" alt="Wat Phra Kaew Murals">
  <p class="photo-details">These murals depict scenes from the Ramakien, Thailand’s national epic, and surround the temple walls, offering visitors insight into Thai mythology and beliefs.</p>

  <h3>Wat Pho (Temple of the Reclining Buddha)</h3>
  <img src="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/e/e3/Wat_Pho_Reclining_Buddha.jpg" alt="Wat Pho Reclining Buddha">
  <p class="photo-details">Wat Pho is famous for its 46-meter-long Reclining Buddha statue, symbolizing the Buddha’s entry into nirvana. It is one of the most photographed religious attractions in Thailand.</p>
  <img src="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/0e/Wat_Pho_Thai_Massage_School.jpg" alt="Thai Massage School Wat Pho">
  <p class="photo-details">This photo shows the traditional Thai massage school at Wat Pho, where tourists can enjoy authentic Thai massage and wellness treatments.</p>

  <h3>Wat Arun (Temple of Dawn)</h3>
  <img src="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/5/5f/Wat_Arun_Ratchawararam.jpg" alt="Wat Arun">
  <p class="photo-details">Wat Arun stands on the west bank of the Chao Phraya River. Its central tower is decorated with colorful porcelain and offers panoramic views of Bangkok.</p>
  <img src="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/2/2e/Wat_Arun_Sunset.jpg" alt="Wat Arun Sunset">
